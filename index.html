How the number gets here.

Short version: a script reads menus every week, and a person fixes what it gets wrong.

The sweep

A weekly job walks online ordering platforms for every Milwaukee-area venue it can reach, pulls the current menu, and keeps the lines that are actually an espresso martini or a Spotted Cow pour. Bottled liqueur and six-packs do not count.

The hand-checks

Plenty of good bars have no online menu. Those are verified by hand from a posted menu or a phone call and merged in, with the source recorded. When a platform and a hand-check disagree, the live platform price wins.

What a price means

It is the menu price before tax and tip, for the standard pour. Happy-hour numbers appear only where the venue posts one — a bar can run a happy hour that does not touch these drinks, and that is noted on the spot.

What it is not

It is a sweep, not a guarantee. Menus change, platforms lag, and the register is the final word. If something here is wrong, it is worth more to fix than to trust.
