How the number gets here.

The sweep. A weekly job walks the ordering platforms every Milwaukee-area venue publishes to, pulls the live menu, and keeps only the lines that are actually an espresso martini or a Spotted Cow pour. Bottled liqueur, six-packs and a chaser tacked onto something else do not count.

The hand-checks. Plenty of good bars never put a menu online. Those are read off a posted menu or confirmed by phone and merged in with the source recorded. Where a live platform and a hand-check disagree, the platform wins.

What a price is. The menu price before tax and tip, for the standard pour. A happy-hour number appears only where the bar posts one — some bars run a happy hour that does not touch these drinks, and that is written on the spot.

What it is not. A sweep, not a promise. Menus change, platforms lag, and the register has the last word. If something here is wrong it is worth more to fix than to trust.
